# Community Code of Conduct

Maos VPN is a technical project open to users and contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence.
- Protect other people's credentials and personal information.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, deliberate exposure of private information, spam, and repeated disruptive behavior are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community or project.

To report conduct privately, use a private GitHub Security Advisory and clearly mark the report as a community conduct matter. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
